Self-taught

I am a self-taught filmmaker from Bhoa, Pathankot, Punjab. From childhood, storytelling through cinema has been my calling — not just as a craft, but as a way of life.

I write, direct, shoot, and edit my own films. My debut short film "Don't Open the Door" was shot, directed, and edited by me in just two days — and earned 946 views organically.

The name Frame Flow came from a moment of pure instinct — I made a frame with my own hands, felt it, and understood: this is how I see life. Every moment flows. Every moment is a frame.

Currently applying to AAFT Noida — B.Sc Cinema — to sharpen my craft and build a team that shares this vision.

Vision

"When you are aware, everything around you is already a shot waiting to be taken. When you are not, even the most beautiful view means nothing. I want to make films that make people THINK — not just watch."

Cinema without meaning is just noise. My goal is to build a body of work that is intelligent, layered, and unforgettable — films that respect the audience's mind the way Christopher Nolan does.
